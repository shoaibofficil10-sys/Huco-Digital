The interactive HUCO sculpture uses Infinite, 3D Head Scan by Lee Perry-Smith, based on work at Triplegangers, under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported License. The mesh is distributed through the Three.js examples.

Adaptations for HUCO Digital include an articulated neck and head, graphite materials, a branded red visor, sculptural fins and interactive lighting.
